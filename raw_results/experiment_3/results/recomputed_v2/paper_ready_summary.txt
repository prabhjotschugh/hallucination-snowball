FOR SECTION 5 (Experiments):
  "We evaluate three pipeline configurations across 140 FinanceBench
   cases with 346 injected hallucinations. Using presence-based HSR --
   whether the injected value persists in the final output -- the
   vanilla pipeline yields 60.7% survival. End-Check, which applies
   identical verification tools to the final output only, achieves
   58.4% -- effectively no improvement. This confirms that by
   Stage 4, hallucinations have been laundered into derived computations
   and narrative prose, rendering even tool-based verification
   ineffective. Our method, which places tool-based verification gates
   at every inter-agent boundary, achieves 16.2% HSR -- a
   42.2 pp improvement over End-Check. Both methods use
   identical tools; the difference is WHERE verification occurs.
   Early verification catches hallucinations while they are still
   checkable facts: Gate 1 alone refutes 0.0% of injections,
   while the full three-gate protocol catches 0.0%. The
   propagation analysis shows injected values dropping from 97%
   presence at Stage 1 to 16% at Stage 4 under our method,
   compared to 97% -> 61% for vanilla."
